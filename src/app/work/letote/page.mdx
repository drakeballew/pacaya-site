## Overview

Le Tote engaged us to help them transition away from Customer.io and onto Iterable. Their email marketing manager had just left the company and they were on a deadline.

We stepped in and pushed the migration forward, starting with a pixel-perfect update of their email templates and then moving on to their automated messaging workflows, where we helped the team spec requirements and work with Engineering to get the necessary data into Segment.
